# Code of Conduct

This project should be a respectful, practical, and welcoming place for people
building Android development tooling.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and tradeoffs.
* Keep discussions focused on the work.
* Give actionable feedback.
* Be patient with new contributors and new Android developers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
* Personal attacks or repeated hostile comments.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Sharing secrets, credentials, or sensitive vulnerability details publicly.
* Deliberately disrupting issues, pull requests, or maintainer workflows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or block users when behavior is harmful to the project or community.

Security issues should be reported through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not through public comments.
